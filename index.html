Most traders lose money without realising it.

Not because their goods didn't sell — but because they never knew their real cost. The flight. The hotel. The customs fee. The exchange rate that shifted while you were still in China. The packaging you forgot to count. Every one of these costs money. Every one of them silently eats your profit. TraderCalc fixes that.
