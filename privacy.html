Legal · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026 · Applies to the TraderCalc mobile application, published by Hakav Enterprise Limited
Plain-language summary TraderCalc is essentially a private local calculator. The only network activity is a live exchange-rate fetch from a public API. No user data ever leaves your device. No accounts. No tracking. No ads.

01 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TraderCalc ("the App"), developed and published by Hakav Enterprise Limited ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the App. We built TraderCalc to work entirely on your device, so there is very little for a privacy policy to say — but we've laid it out in full below.

02 Data we do not collect

TraderCalc does not require an account, and does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

Not collected

  • No user accounts or login
  • No email addresses
  • No names
  • No phone numbers
  • No location or GPS data
  • No camera or photo library access
  • No contacts list access
  • No advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • No analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • No remarketing or tracking pixels
  • No data shared with third parties for commercial purposes
  • No children's data — the App is for adults 18 and over

What actually happens

  • All calculations are stored locally on your device only, using on-device storage — never transmitted to any server
  • The App fetches live currency rates from exchangerate-api.com — a read-only request that contains zero user data
  • Your subscription purchase is handled entirely by Google Play — TraderCalc never sees or stores payment information

03 The one exception: live exchange rates

To convert between currencies, TraderCalc makes an outbound request to exchangerate-api.com to fetch current exchange rates. This is a read-only query for public rate data — it does not include your name, device identifier, location, or any other personal information. It simply asks "what is today's rate" and receives an answer.

04 Payments

TraderCalc is available as a monthly or annual subscription purchase through Google Play. All payment processing, billing information, and transaction records are handled directly by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. We never receive, see, or store your card details, billing address, or any other payment information.

05 Children's privacy (COPPA)

TraderCalc is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the App does not collect personal information from any user, this applies equally to users of any age. The App is intended for adults 18 and over, consistent with its purpose as a business and trading tool.

06 Your rights under GDPR (European users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, delete, or port any personal data held about you, and the right to object to or restrict its processing. Because TraderCalc does not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, delete, or transfer on your behalf — the only data that exists lives locally on your own device, under your own control, and is removed automatically if you uninstall the App.

07 Your rights under CCPA / CPRA (California users)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), gives you the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to request its deletion, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. TraderCalc does not collect, sell, or share personal information, so there is no data associated with you for us to disclose, delete, or stop selling. We do not use "sale" or "sharing" of data as those terms are defined under CCPA/CPRA in any part of the App.

08 Data retention & deletion

Because all data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. Deleting your calculations within the App, or uninstalling the App entirely, permanently removes all associated data — there is no copy stored anywhere else for us to retain or delete.

09 Changes to this policy

If we ever change how TraderCalc handles data — for example, if we introduce an optional cloud backup feature in the future — we will update this page and revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this policy occasionally, particularly before granting the App any new permissions.

10 Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how TraderCalc works, reach us at info@tradercalc.net.
